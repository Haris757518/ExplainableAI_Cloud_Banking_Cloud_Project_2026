# Live faculty demonstration

Open http://127.0.0.1:8012/ and refresh.

1. **Run test** collects a fresh normal baseline after the eight AWS banking services are ready. Wait for Normal.
2. Choose **Database lock** or **CPU pressure**, then click **Inject fault**. Both are bounded to about 30 seconds and restore automatically. CPU pressure reduces the ledgerwriter allowance and introduces a bounded competing process inside that exact research container cgroup; an independent systemd timer also restores the allowance.
3. The graph plots measured live latency and the frozen threshold. After an anomaly, the frozen earlier banking GNN ranks current measured resource errors. PROBE-XAI then inspects current cgroup allowance/throttling and PostgreSQL blocking/lock evidence. Its supported cause may differ from the GNN symptom rank.
4. Wait for Normal, then click **Verify recovery**. This pauses writes and checks both account balances against committed ledger records, returned-history ID uniqueness and absence of database blocking. It does not prove every historical row or every uncertain request outcome.
5. Click **Run test** to resume payments after verification.

Missing data is not substituted. Stale data is marked Paused. Findings retain their observation time after a fault clears. Deeper actor/configuration-change attribution remains unavailable without audit records. The live sampling cadence differs from the earlier training experiment; the live demonstration is not a new accuracy benchmark or a worldwide novelty claim.

The user approved US$10 total and up to six aggregate running hours, replacing the prior US$5/two-hour limit. The exact research instance is guarded by its account and ResearchRun tag. The pre-existing payment server is excluded. The persistent guest guard stops compute before the six-hour ceiling. No restart automation was created. After the review, export the evidence and terminate the exact research instance when the user requests cleanup; stopped root-disk storage persists until termination.

Operational state, raw measurements, action receipts and verification evidence are retained locally under results/runtime/live-aws/. This folder is ignored by Git. The read-only bridge retries transient AWS collection failures; the local dashboard mutations require a loopback client and matching local Origin, with fixed commands only.
